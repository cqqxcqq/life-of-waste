PLASTIC WASTE · 2019

9%recycled

Only 9% of global plastic waste was ultimately recycled in 2019. The remainder was landfilled, incinerated or mismanaged.

Source: OECD, Global Plastics Outlook ↗

03 / WHY IT MATTERS

The real cost of waste

Improperly managed waste affects ecosystems, our climate, and valuable resources.

≈

Water & wildlife

Plastic pollution can reach rivers and oceans, where it can entangle wildlife or be mistaken for food.

◉

Climate

Organic waste decomposing in landfills can release methane, a greenhouse gas.

↻

Natural resources

Making products from recovered materials can reduce the need for some virgin raw materials.

04 / SMALL ACTIONS

Reduce. Reuse. Recycle.

Preventing waste comes first. When recycling, check what your local collection programme accepts.

01

Reduce

Avoid unnecessary packaging and single-use purchases.

02

Reuse

Repair, donate or refill usable items before discarding them.

03

Recycle

Sort accepted, clean and dry recyclables according to local rules.
